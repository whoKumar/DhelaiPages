About the book

You don't need to be brilliant. You just need to be early.

The world changes in waves. Some people notice early. Some people notice when the wave has already passed.

The Agricultural Wave. The Industrial Wave. The Internet. Mobile. Social Media. The Creator Economy. And now — AI.

This book is not a technical guide. It is not a prediction. It is a pattern-recognition manual — a wake-up call, a playbook, a compass. Whether you are a student, a working professional, a homemaker, a shopkeeper, or someone who feels left behind by the speed of change — this book is for you.
