Certificate Terms

Last updated: 29 May 2026

Dhelai issues course-completion certificates when you pass the quiz for a course in the app. These terms explain how the name works, why generation is one-time, how certificates are verified, and when one can be revoked. By generating a certificate you accept these terms.

1. Your name on the certificate

Enter your name exactly as it appears on your 10th marksheet or Aadhaar card. If you are younger and do not yet have a 10th marksheet, use the name on your Aadhaar card or other official ID. We do not verify your name or identity — we simply print what you enter, so the accuracy of the name is entirely your responsibility.

2. One-time generation — the name is permanent

Generating your certificate is a one-time action. Once generated, the name is permanent and cannot be changed later — exactly like the name printed on your 10th marksheet. The same name is used across all certificates you earn on Dhelai. Please double-check the spelling before you confirm.

3. Revocation & deletion

A certificate may be revoked or deleted if the details entered are found to be legally incorrect, fraudulent, or used to impersonate another person. A revoked certificate will show as "Revoked" on the public verification page.

4. Public verification

Every certificate carries a permanent Certificate ID and a QR code linking to https://dhelai.in/verify.html. Anyone can scan the code or open the link to confirm the certificate ID, holder name, course, level, and issue date. This makes the certificate a real, checkable credential you can share on LinkedIn, a résumé, or with an employer.

5. What the certificate is — and isn't

A Dhelai certificate recognises that you completed a specific course and passed its quiz inside the Dhelai app. It is not a government-recognised degree, diploma, or accredited qualification, and it does not certify professional licensure. It is a proof of learning and effort.

6. Privacy

To make verification work, your certificate holder name, course, level, issue date, and Certificate ID are stored in our database. This is covered by our Privacy Policy. You can request deletion of your account and certificates at any time — see Delete your account.

7. Changes

If we change these terms materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Certificates already issued keep their original Certificate ID and remain verifiable.

8. Contact

Questions about a certificate? Email dhelai.mail@gmail.com.
